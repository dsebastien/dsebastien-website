# Compost basics

A heap needs green matter, brown matter, air and water.

- Turn the heap every two weeks.
- Keep it as wet as a wrung sponge.
- Water it from the [[Rain barrel]] when the summer is dry.

The herbs that like compost are in [[10 Garden/Herb bed/Herb bed|the herb bed]].
