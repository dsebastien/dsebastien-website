## Issue 49: the findings of the third review

Branch `ticket/49-note-types`. Decision R61 (finding 7 decided by the author).

### Done

The ten findings of round 3 (`2026-11-21.md`), each with a test first:

1. One tag reader for the markup, the properties panel and the lists (`apps/site/src/tags.ts`). A tag of the text that the parser reads but that has no page (`#tag/`, `#a//b`, `#a·b`) stays plain text. `MARKUP_VERSION` is 8. The check of the built site now finds a link of any page that leads to the "not found" page (`fileOfLink()` gives a page, a missing page, or something else: a file, an attachment, another site).
2. A tag in two Unicode forms is one tag page: `tagPath()` and the tag levels use the folded form.
3. A tag page names the tag as most of its notes write it (`#PKM`), and so do the links to its nested tags.
4. A shared description with the title in front is cut to 155 characters again.
5. A `description` property gives the text of its links, not their Markdown.
6. The description of a note of a media type is the sentence of its facts alone (IDX-06).
7. Decided by the author: a type hub of fewer than 5 notes and `/-/other` say `noindex, follow`; the library is indexed as a curated list. IDX-22 says it.
8. The library has the H1 "Library"; the pages of the backlinks, the folders, the choices and the 404 page have the site name in their titles. A test reads the title of every built HTML file.
9. The nested tags show on page 1 of a tag only.
10. The type badge is a link with a hover style (`badge-link`) and the name "Movie: all Movies".

### Decisions

- The author (R61, finding 7): the floor of 5 notes for a type hub, `/-/other` not indexed, the library indexed.
- Readings of the assistant: two generated pages with one title stop the build also when they are not indexed; the accessible name of the badge starts with its visible text (WCAG 2.5.3).

### Review

One review of the commit of these corrections (the rule of three rounds per ticket was done): no blocker, and no way for a note that is not published to reach the site. Its nine points are GitHub issue 98. The largest: a raw HTML anchor with a root-relative address that leads to no page would stop the build through the new link check. Measured on the 20,734 published notes (read only, counts only): no such anchor, and no raw anchor with the class `tag`.
