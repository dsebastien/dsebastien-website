# The main site is the original of everything that is also published there

Some notes also exist as posts on the main site of the author: articles, newsletters, and notes that the Ghost Publish plugin sends there as news posts. For search engines, the post on the main site is the original: the note on the notes site names the URL of the post in its canonical link. The author publishes in his notes first, and they are the root of everything, but his main domain is the main site.

Considered: the direction that the main site states today, where a news post names the note as the original, and only articles and newsletters name themselves. The assistant recommended to keep it, because nothing had to change. The author chose one rule for all.

Consequence: the notes that were sent as news posts hand their search ranking to the main site, and a part of them are notes that bring search traffic to the notes site today. The author accepted this. The Ghost Publish plugin and the existing news posts must change before or at the switch, never after: if each site names the other as the original, search engines get a loop. The notes site needs the real URL of each post, and the switch gate tests that both sites name the same URL. A note stays served under its vault path (ADR 0005).
